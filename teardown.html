Desk shop · Aug 25, 2026

What happens after 6pm

A Tuesday-night desk shop of 12 HVAC shops around Fate, Rockwall, Royse City, Wylie, and Forney. This is not a phone log. We did not call anyone. We opened what a homeowner opens after dinner: the shop’s own site, the hours it posts, and the form it leaves you with. HVAC is the example. A furniture floor, an insurance desk, a med spa after 6 look the same.

What we found

12 shops. The pattern is the point.

  • 12 HVAC shops. Nobody named. Quotes are public.
  • 10 advertise 24/7, all-hours, or “call us anytime” emergency service.
  • 6 of those 10 post hours that close. After close, a form.
  • 3 are the textbook version: 24/7 on the homepage, 8-to-6 in the footer, “we’ll call within one business day” on the form.

The other four either actually answer after hours (phone, live answering, or a same-number text-back) or they never claimed 24/7 in the first place.

The leak, in one picture

A promise on the homepage and a next-business-day form after close.

That is the leak. Not a bad review. Not a voicemail we heard.

  1. 1

    A Wylie shop, on a single page

    Homepage: “We’re available 24/7 and offer same-day service.” Footer: Mon–Fri 8am–6pm. Saturday until 3. Sunday closed. The form: “We’ll call within 1 business day.”

  2. 2

    A Rockwall shop, in two pages

    Homepage: “available at all hours for heating and cooling emergencies.” Contact page: “call us during normal business hours, or use this convenient contact form.” Their manufacturer dealer listing says Monday–Friday, 7 to 5.

  3. 3

    A Fate shop

    Puts “24-Hour Emergency Service” and “Call Us Anytime” over footer hours of 8 to 6.

  4. 4

    Another Rockwall shop

    Advertises 24/7 emergency HVAC, then prints on every coupon, including the emergency-repair coupon: “Not valid afterhours, weekends, or holidays.”

Who does it right

Three shops on the same list already split the two paths in writing.

  1. A

    Live answering

    The phone is 24/7 live answering. The form is same-business-day.

  2. B

    Call or text

    Same number, day or night. No overtime charge.

  3. C

    Call for repair

    They tell you to call. Not to wait on the form.

The gap is not “HVAC is bad at phones.” The gap is a 24/7 badge with no after-hours path behind it. A furniture floor that closes at 6 and a contact form that says “we’ll reach out next business day” is the same page with different pictures.

What we did not find

No invented voicemail. No invented review.

We found no public review, for any of these 12, that described an after-hours call, a voicemail, or a missing callback. We are not going to invent one. If the leak is real, it will show up in the first two weeks of a missed-call count, not in a quote we made up.

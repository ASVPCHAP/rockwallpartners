The three leaks

Jobs walk when three things slip.

I do not start with tools. I start with what the business is already losing.

  1. 1

    Missed calls

    Lunch. After 5. Someone is with a client. The next business picks up, and that job does not come back.

  2. 2

    Slow follow-up

    A web form or leftover quote sits until tonight. They already booked someone else.

  3. 3

    No reviews, no-shows

    Good jobs never get asked for a review. Reminders do not go out. The calendar has holes.
